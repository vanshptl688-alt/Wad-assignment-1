01 — PROFILE

About Arvind Krishna

Arvind Krishna is the Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer of IBM, one of the world's leading technology companies. He joined IBM in 1990 and has spent more than three decades working across research, software, systems, cloud and business leadership. Before becoming CEO in April 2020, he served as Senior Vice President for Cloud and Cognitive Software and as Director of IBM Research. Krishna was a principal architect of IBM's $34 billion acquisition of Red Hat in 2019, a major strategic move that strengthened IBM's position in hybrid cloud. His leadership has helped focus IBM on artificial intelligence, hybrid cloud, enterprise software, cybersecurity and quantum computing. Earlier in his career, he contributed to wireless networking, security, systems and databases, and helped establish IBM's security software business. He earned an undergraduate degree from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur, and a Ph.D. in electrical engineering from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. His career combines scientific research, technology strategy and large-scale business transformation, making him an important figure in modern enterprise technology.
